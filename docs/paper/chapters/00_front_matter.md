# 基于端侧 AI 的银行零售反欺诈与反洗钱风险多智能体预警系统

—— 面向 AMD 锐龙 AI MAX+ 平台的本地化金融风控智能体实践

## 摘要

银行零售业务中的反欺诈、反洗钱和贷前风控需要同时处理客户身份、账户交易、设备登录、商户关系、投诉线索和历史风险事件等多源敏感数据。传统规则系统能够识别部分显性风险，但面对分拆交易、虚拟币资金归集、异地新设备登录、贷款包装流水、共享设备团伙操作等组合型风险时，仍然依赖人工跨系统检索和证据串联，研判效率较低。与此同时，大语言模型具备较强的文本理解、推理和报告生成能力，但金融数据具有高敏感性和强合规约束，若直接上传云端模型，可能带来隐私泄露、审计困难、网络依赖和合规风险。因此，构建面向银行内网和本地工作站环境的端侧 AI 风控智能体，具有明确的现实意义。

本文设计并实现了 Sentinel Edge 银行零售反欺诈与反洗钱风险多智能体预警系统。系统基于 AMD 锐龙 AI MAX+ 平台的 CPU、集成 Radeon GPU、Ryzen AI NPU 与统一内存架构，构建“本地模型推理、黑名单过滤、向量暂存、关系图谱、多维风险评分、趋势预测、人工复核”的端到端闭环。其中，Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 以 Q6_K_M 量化形式通过 llama.cpp 在显卡侧运行，BGE M3 Embedding 与 BGE Reranker v2-m3 通过 vLLM 在 NPU 侧推理，CPU 负责智能体调度、规则逻辑、数据库访问和 OpenAI 兼容接口调用。系统首先对输入金融事件进行结构化标准化，然后通过本地黑名单与高危规则完成前置过滤；未命中高危规则的事件按低风险展示，并写入向量暂存池作为后续历史上下文；命中规则或风险信号的事件进入事件分类、关系图谱构建、多维风险评估和趋势预测流程。针对高风险事件，系统会回捞同客户、同账户或语义相关历史事件，进行增量图谱补全和二次研判，从而提升对组合型风险链路的识别能力。

本文的创新点包括：第一，提出面向银行零售风控场景的端侧多智能体协同架构，将规则系统、向量召回、关系图谱和本地大模型推理结合到统一流程中；第二，设计面向金融事件的多维风险评分方法，从客户身份、交易行为、交易对手、金额频次、设备地域、历史上下文和合规信号等维度计算风险分数；第三，构建低风险事件暂存与高风险事件回捞机制，将原本容易被忽略的普通事件转化为后续组合风险识别的上下文证据；第四，围绕 AMD 锐龙 AI MAX+ 平台设计 CPU、GPU、NPU 与统一内存的协同使用方案，使显卡侧大模型生成、NPU 侧向量与重排推理、CPU 侧智能体编排能够在端侧协同完成。系统实现了风险分析、事件库、事件详情、人物图谱、黑名单管理、系统状态与总览页面，为银行内网环境下的智能风控辅助研判提供了一种可落地方案。

## Abstract

Retail banking anti-fraud, anti-money laundering, and pre-loan risk control require the joint analysis of sensitive data from customer identities, account transactions, device logins, merchant relationships, complaint clues, and historical risk events. Traditional rule-based systems can identify explicit risk signals, but they still rely heavily on manual cross-system search and evidence connection when facing composite risks such as transaction splitting, cryptocurrency-related fund aggregation, abnormal device login, fabricated loan cash flow, and shared-device fraud groups. Large language models provide strong capabilities in text understanding, reasoning, and report generation. However, directly uploading financial data to cloud-based models may introduce privacy leakage, audit difficulty, network dependency, and compliance risks. Therefore, building an edge-side AI risk control agent system for bank intranet and local workstation environments is of practical significance.

This paper designs and implements Sentinel Edge, a multi-agent early-warning system for retail banking anti-fraud and anti-money laundering. Based on the AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ platform with CPU, integrated Radeon GPU, Ryzen AI NPU, and unified memory architecture, the system builds an end-to-end workflow that integrates local model inference, blacklist filtering, vector-based temporary storage, relationship graph construction, multidimensional risk scoring, trend prediction, and human review. In the local inference stack, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B is deployed with Q6_K_M quantization through llama.cpp on the GPU, while BGE M3 Embedding and BGE Reranker v2-m3 are served by vLLM on the NPU; the CPU handles agent orchestration, rule logic, database access, and OpenAI-compatible API calls. The system first normalizes raw financial event text into structured fields, and then performs local blacklist and high-risk rule filtering. Events that do not match high-risk rules are displayed as low-risk and stored in a vector pool as historical context. Events that match risk rules or risk signals enter the full pipeline of event classification, relationship graph construction, multidimensional risk assessment, and trend prediction. For high-risk events, the system recalls historical events related by customer, account, or semantic similarity, performs incremental graph completion, and triggers secondary assessment to improve the identification of composite risk chains.

The contributions of this work are fourfold. First, it proposes an edge-side multi-agent architecture for retail banking risk control, integrating rule systems, vector retrieval, relationship graphs, and local LLM inference into a unified workflow. Second, it designs a multidimensional financial risk scoring method based on customer identity, transaction behavior, counterparty risk, amount and velocity, device and geolocation, historical context, and compliance signals. Third, it introduces a mechanism that temporarily stores low-risk events and recalls them when high-risk events appear, turning ordinary events into contextual evidence for composite risk detection. Fourth, it designs a hardware-aware deployment scheme for AMD Ryzen AI MAX+, coordinating GPU-side LLM generation, NPU-side embedding and reranking inference, CPU-side agent orchestration, and unified memory for local analysis, visualization, and review of sensitive financial data. The system provides risk analysis, event repository, event detail, person graph, blacklist management, system status, and dashboard pages, offering a practical solution for intelligent risk control assistance in bank intranet environments.

**Keywords**: Edge AI; Financial Risk Control; Anti-Fraud and AML; Multi-Agent; Relationship Graph
